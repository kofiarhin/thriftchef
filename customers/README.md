# Customer Evidence

Store only real, attributable customer evidence here: interview notes, support messages, usability observations, survey responses, and repeated customer language.

## Evidence record

Each note should include:

- date and source;
- customer segment and relevant context;
- exact language where permission allows;
- observed pain, workaround, objection, or desired outcome;
- frequency or confidence without exaggeration;
- product implication as a hypothesis, not a fact;
- consent, privacy, and redaction notes;
- follow-up question or experiment.

## Rules

- Do not invent interviews, quotes, segments, demand, or validation.
- Remove credentials, addresses, payment data, health data, and unnecessary personally identifying information.
- Distinguish direct quotes from summaries and team interpretation.
- Do not let one anecdote override catalogue integrity, safety, or retailer-isolation requirements.
- Promote repeated evidence into `context/product.md` or `roadmap.md` only through an approved documentation ticket.
